Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I place an order?

1.1 Browse or Search for Products

Use the navigation menu or the search bar to find the items you wish to purchase.

1.2 Select Quantity and Add to Cart

Choose the desired options (e.g., size, color, etc.) and select the quantity you want. Click the "Add to Cart" button to add the product to your shopping cart. You can continue shopping or view your cart at any time by clicking the cart icon at the top of the page.

1.3 Review Your Cart

Once you're ready to complete your purchase, click on the shopping cart icon to view the items you've selected. Review the items in your cart, make any necessary changes (e.g., update quantity or remove items), and ensure everything is correct. Click "Proceed to Checkout" when you're ready to place the order.

2. What payment methods are available?

We offer a variety of secure and convenient payment methods to make your shopping experience smooth and hassle-free. You can choose from the following options:

2.1 Credit/Debit Cards

We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. Simply enter your card details at checkout for a secure transaction.

2.2 Net Banking

For customers who prefer online banking, we offer net banking options for most major banks. Choose your bank during checkout and follow the prompts to complete the payment.

2.3 UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

UPI is a fast and secure payment method that links your bank account to your UPI ID. Enter your UPI ID at checkout and approve the payment through your UPI-enabled app.

2.4 Cash on Delivery (COD)

If you prefer to pay in cash, select the "Cash on Delivery" option at checkout. You can pay for your order upon delivery. Please note that COD may not be available for all products or locations.

3. How can I track my order?

Tracking your order is easy! Follow these steps:

3.1 Check Your Email

After placing your order, you’ll receive a confirmation email with a tracking link. Click the link to view the status of your shipment.

3.2 Log in to Your Account

Go to the “My Orders” section in your account to see the current status and tracking details of your order.

4. What is the return policy?

You can return items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Please follow these guidelines:

4.1 Return Condition

You can return items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

4.2 Condition of Items

Items must be unused and in their original packaging, including tags and labels.

4.3 Return Process

To initiate a return, please contact our customer support to receive a return authorization and instructions on how to send back the item.

5. How can I contact customer support?

For any customer queries or customer support, follow any of the steps given below:

5.1 Email Us

Send your inquiries to our customer support email at support@sail.com. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

5.2 Phone Support
